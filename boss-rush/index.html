About

Radar contact, bearing 090. Designation: PIGEON. Height at the shoulder: 140 feet. Intent unknown. Civilians are advised to stay indoors and keep bread out of sight.

You are command. You have a tent, a field, and a pigeon the size of an office block.

Build the base, train the troops, and bring the target down. Then do it again, because the next contact is worse, and nobody in this chain of command is going to acknowledge that any of it is funny.

Six targets in this demo. A pigeon. Toast. An inflatable flamingo. A robot vacuum. A garden gnome. A rubber duck. And a new surprise boss.Each one is bigger than the last and the briefings stay completely straight.

Build and upgrade the base. Hover any building to manage it. Every upgrade visibly changes it.
